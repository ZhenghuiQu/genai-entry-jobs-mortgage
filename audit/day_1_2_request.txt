# ROLE: Senior Empirical Research Engineer and Reproducibility Auditor

Project: Generative AI, Entry-Level Employment, and US Mortgage Markets.

Repository:
https://github.com/ZhenghuiQu/genai-entry-jobs-mortgage

We have completed two independent literature reviews (GPT and Claude) and a subsequent methodological cross-review.

Your immediate assignment is **Day 1–2 Data Feasibility Validation**, not full empirical implementation.

## 1. Read the research documents

Inspect the original research assignment, both independent literature-review packages, and the cross-review documents.

There are two different cross-review versions:

- The complete GPT–Claude cross-review, which provisionally recommends a commuting-zone-level design.
- An independent Claude-only audit, which recommends a simpler state-level design.

Do not silently merge these into a single specification.

Treat CZ as the provisional preferred geography and State as a pre-specified feasibility fallback. Do not finalize this choice until the geographic and exposure-construction checks are completed.

If any required research documents are missing from the repository, explicitly identify them. Do not invent their contents.

## 2. Conduct the critical feasibility checks

### Gate A — HMDA Data

Verify official programmatic access to HMDA static snapshot data for 2018–2025.

Inspect schemas and a small reproducibly downloaded sample from early and late years.

Check the availability, type, and observed codes of:

- activity_year;
- applicant_age;
- action_taken;
- loan_purpose;
- lien_status;
- occupancy_type;
- construction_method;
- total_units;
- loan_type;
- reverse_mortgage;
- open-end_line_of_credit;
- business_or_commercial_purpose;
- county_code;
- state_code;
- lei.

Verify the treatment of missing, exempt, and non-applicable values.

Document year-specific schema differences.

Do not launch a full national download until the acquisition procedure and resource requirements have been validated.

### Gate B — Occupational AI Exposure

Inspect the original Eloundou et al. occupation-level data, including `dv_rating_beta`.

Verify:

- The original occupational classification;
- The availability of official occupation crosswalks;
- The 2015–2019 ACS PUMS occupation coding;
- Treatment of aggregated occupation codes containing X or Y;
- Matching coverage under a documented mapping;
- Whether employment-weighted exposure can be constructed for ages 22–34 and 25–34.

Do not use undocumented prefix matching as a substitute for an occupational concordance.

Report employment-weighted match coverage, ambiguous mappings, and unmatched occupational groups.

### Gate C — Geographic Crosswalks

Inspect the official Dorn commuting-zone crosswalk files.

Verify:

- County-to-1990-CZ mappings;
- 2010 PUMA-to-CZ allocation factors;
- Unique geographic identifiers;
- Historical county-code changes;
- Connecticut geographic reporting changes;
- Coverage for the intended HMDA sample.

Report unmatched geography, allocation errors, and coverage percentages.

Do not silently drop unmatched observations or introduce arbitrary geographic matches.

### Gate D — Auxiliary Data

Verify the availability and coding of:

- Census QWI age-specific employment and hiring;
- ACS baseline affordability variables;
- 2019 population weights;
- Required regional characteristics.

Check QWI coverage and missingness, including the previously identified Michigan limitation.

Do not begin the full labor-market mechanism analysis.

### Gate E — Computational Feasibility

Estimate:

- Required download size;
- Expected local storage needs;
- Peak memory considerations;
- The feasibility of streaming HMDA aggregation using DuckDB or another suitable framework;
- Expected bottlenecks on the current machine.

Use small reproducible tests rather than speculative performance estimates.

## 3. Design integrity

Do not estimate the primary treatment coefficient or inspect treatment-effect statistical significance during this feasibility stage.

Do not select between CZ and State based on regression results.

Preserve the existing proposed age groups, treatment timing, and outcome definitions as provisional specifications.

Report any internal inconsistencies between the available cross-review documents.

Explicitly distinguish:

- Verified data facts;
- Technical assumptions;
- Unresolved methodological choices;
- Recommendations.

Create:

`docs/data_source_registry.md`

- Limitations;

Include reproducible scripts for the tests actually performed.

## 5. Stopping rule

Stop after completing the critical feasibility checks.

Do not begin the national full-scale regression pipeline, robustness analysis, or paper writing without a subsequent implementation instruction.

At the end, recommend one of:

A. CZ primary design is technically feasible.

B. CZ is not reliably feasible within the project deadline; use the pre-specified State alternative.

C. A critical data dependency remains unresolved and needs further work.

Explain the recommendation using observed feasibility evidence, not expected statistical significance.

Commit the verified scripts, documentation, and results to the project repository with an informative commit message.

Preserve all research and AI-interaction audit trails.
